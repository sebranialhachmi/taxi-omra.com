# 08 · Winning Strategy — Jeddah Travels (أسفار جدة)

## Positioning

**The bilingual, pilgrim-and-VIP transport platform for Jeddah — measurably faster, more informative, and more trustworthy than any KSA taxi site.**

Three non-negotiables:
1. **Bilingual parity.** Every public page ships in AR and EN with reciprocal `hreflang` + `x-default`. No auto-translation.
2. **Topical authority in the airport ↔ holy cities cluster.** Own KAIA terminals, Makkah/Madinah districts, Miqat points, and every commercially relevant route pair.
3. **Trust through transparency.** Published price bands, on-time SLA, verified reviews with schema, clear cancellation policy.

## Differentiators vs. the 11 competitors

| Axis | Best competitor | Jeddah Travels edge |
|---|---|---|
| Route pages | Almost none | Programmatic route hub with Trip schema |
| Airport pages | Single generic page | Per-terminal, Airport schema |
| Schema | LocalBusiness only | Full stack incl. FAQPage, Trip, Vehicle, Article |
| Bilingual | Partial | Reciprocal hreflang + x-default, translated bodies |
| Performance | Mid-tier | Green CWV on 4G mobile — enforced budget |
| Trust | Fleet images | Reviews schema, SLA, price bands, policy pages |
| Booking | WhatsApp handoff | On-site form → validated → thank-you + deep-link |
| Design | Templated luxury | Editorial Noir & Gold system, RTL/LTR verified |
| Blog | Missing/stale | Editorial cadence with Article schema and internal links |
| Governance | Ad-hoc | Decision checklist gate (§09) |

## KPI targets (per axis, beating the current best competitor)

- SEO: ≥ 9 vs. C3's 7.
- Performance (CWV mobile 4G, p75): LCP < 2.0 s, INP < 200 ms, CLS < 0.05.
- Content: ≥ 800 words per landing page, unique bilingual.
- Trust: ≥ 20 reviews with schema, published SLA, policies live.
- Schema: 100% of public routes emit valid JSON-LD (CI-verified).
- Booking: on-site form conversion measured; WhatsApp fallback preserved.

## What we deliberately will NOT copy

- Yellow/black taxi cliché (C2).
- Overloaded mega-menus (C2, C9).
- WhatsApp-only funnels with no on-site form (C1, C4, C6, C8, C11).
- Stock imagery clutter (C1, C4, C5, C9).
- Monolithic sitemaps and LocalBusiness-only schema (all).

## Execution guardrail

Every design, SEO, and dev decision must clear the **§09 checklist** before merge. If it does not beat the current best competitor on the relevant axis, iterate until it does.
